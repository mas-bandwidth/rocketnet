# Credits

rocketnet is built by Glenn Fiedler, with a team of AI collaborators helping write, review, test and document the library. Different models bring different blind spots. The useful part is putting their work through independent review and real tests.

## Our sponsor

Thank you to **Dean Hall at RocketWerkz** for sponsoring the library. rocketnet is named in his honor. We hope you build some cool games with it!
